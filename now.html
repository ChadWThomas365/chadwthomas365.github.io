What is a "nowpage"?

According to Derek, "Most websites have a link that says “about” that tells you the background of this person. It’s called an “about page”. Most websites have a link that says “contact” that tells you how to contact this person. It’s called a “contact page”. Personal websites often have a link that says “now” that tells you what this person is focused on at this point in their life. It’s called a “now page”. See examples by browsing nownownow.com. The URL is usually “/now”, like it’s usually “/about” and “/contact” in those examples, but the URL could be anything."

In my case, I update my information to have another html file that the Impossible List links to. You can even view these documents my GitHub repository where I store the files! Soooo exciting!
