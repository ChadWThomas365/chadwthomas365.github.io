I Protect Myself

Written by Chad W. Thomas on August 23, 2026

Suicidal thoughts exist because I want to escape from a threat. I feel afraid of people to the point of wanting to run away from them. There is nothing wrong with experiencing suicidal thoughts. Suicidal ideation is a sign of a need for safety. I don’t have to fear thoughts.

    I think thoughts.
    I exist separate from thoughts.
    I am not thoughts.
    What am I?
    I observe thoughts.
    I exist separate from thoughts.
    I am not thoughts.
    I am a spirit with a connection to this body.
    Death is the separation of the spirit from the body.
    Death does not counter evil.
    “You can’t kill a spirit even if you tried to. I’m inside you.”
    Do the best I can to be the person I wish I had to protect me.
    I protect myself by locking my door and window.
    I protect myself by eating peanut butter crackers and drinking water.
    I protect myself by journaling about what happened to process how I experienced what happened and validate how I feel.
    I protect myself by listening to Hip Hop music. I relate to Hip Hop music about poverty, crime, suicidal thoughts and an attempt, feeling lonely and unwanted, a desire to protect someone, and a desire to protect myself.
    I protect myself by practicing yoga to release tension from my body and detach from my body.
    I protect myself by acknowledging I do not need anyone to protect me.
    I protect myself by reminding myself I am enough.
    I am enough.
