Introduction to Chad W. Thomas

Chad W. Thomas researches public health in a behavioral health setting funded by the SAMHSA. Chad has research experience funded by the NIH and FDA with three scholarly publications from the research funded by the NIH. Chad’s current research focuses on connecting people to housing, substance use recovery services, and therapy.

Chad graduated cum laude in three years with a Bachelor of Science in Economics with a minor in Mathematics and a minor in Statistics from Florida State University in 2018.

Chad studies for a Master of Public Health at the University of South Florida with a concentration in Epidemiology. Chad intends to applied his career experience and education to public health policy to expand access to health insurance and health care.

In his free time, he reads philosophy to learn how to improve his integrity and apply lessons from experience with Crohn’s Disease and an impoverished childhood. He strives to be the person he wished he had to protect him during hardship.
