Introduction to an Impossible List

I credit the Impossible List to Thomas Frank at College Info Geek. The purpose of an Impossible List is to list everything you once thought was impossible, then track when you accomplish each goal and build upon prior goals. They can be any kind of hopes, wishes, dreams, imaginary ideas you wished you could do, or anything you were ever told you could never do or were not good enough to do. The more emotionally driven the idea is, the better!

I started an Impossible List in 2021 to practice building habits after I overcame the worst parts of having an ileostomy in 2018 and 2019. I went away from the list as some things got difficult in life, like when Hurricane Ian damaged the roof of where I lived in 2022.

I decided to make a new list after I wrote my memoir You Are Enough: How I Learned to Love Myself

Chad W. Thomas' Impossible List

My impossible list will not consist of things that are beyond my control at any given moment. I will prioritize goals that I can influence.

For example, I can influence whether I graduate from graduate school. I can control whether I apply to graduate programs and my effort to pass courses.

  • Receive an acceptance letter to graduate school. - Accomplished in May, 2026!
  • Start graduate school. - Anticipation: August 24, 2026.
  • Graduate from graduate school with a Master of Public Health

If you asked me about whether I believed I would graduate from graduate school when I had an ileostomy in 2018, I would have looked at you like you were crazy and told you, "I'm lucky to be alive!"

  • Type a memoir. - Accomplished in May and June, 2026!
  • Publish a memoir. - Anticipation: August 3, 2026.
  • Share my memoir with others who could benefit.
  • Sell a copy of my memoir through social media engagement.
