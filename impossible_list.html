Introduction to an Impossible List

I credit the Impossible List to Thomas Frank at College Info Geek. The purpose of an Impossible List is to list everything you once thought was impossible, then track when you accomplish each goal and build upon prior goals. They can be any kind of hopes, wishes, dreams, and imaginary idea you wished you could do. The more emotionally drivven the idea is, the better!

I started an Impossible List ain 2021 to help build habits after I overcame the worst parts of having an ileostomy in 2018 and 2019. I went away from the list as some things got difficult in life, like when Hurricane Ian damaged the roof of where I lived in 2022.

I decided to make a new list after I wrote my memoirYou Are Enough: How I Learned to Love Myself

Chad W. Thomas' Impossible List

My impossible list will not consist of things that are beyond my control at any given moment. I will prioritize goals that I can influence.

    For example, I can influence whether I graduate from graduate school. I can control whether I apply to graduate programs and my effort to pass courses.
